## Why this is the most urgent task

The site's one promise is "which card fits your device and meets spec". Right now it breaks that promise on its own pages:

- **51 recommendations are cards the device can't physically take.** Fifteen full-size SD cameras (Canon G7X III, Fujifilm X100V, Leica M10, Nikon D7500, Sony A7R…) list microSD cards as picks. The Nikon Z9 takes CFexpress only and gets an SD card. The DJI Mavic 3 takes microSD and gets a full-size SD card.
- **25 recommendations are below the minimum the same page states.** The Steam Deck page says "Not below V30" and then lists a V10 card. Several V90 cameras list V60 cards.

These are high-traffic pages, and the error sits right at the "Check Price" button. A reader who buys the pick and gets a card that doesn't fit has every reason to return it and never come back. Everything else in the plan (linking, enrichment) sends more traffic to these pages, so fix them first.

Three smaller correctness jobs are bundled in because they're quick, or because you'll be waiting on Pietro: redirects for removed pages (C0-1), locale spec drift (C0-3), and leftover "tested" claims (C0-4).

## Tasks, in order

### Step 1: Build the recommendation check (2 to 3 hours)

Create `scripts/check-recommendations.js` (npm script `check:recs`). It reads `data/devices.json`, `data/sdcards.json` and `data/promoted-cards.json`, and prints one line per problem, grouped by card. It exits 1 if anything isn't in the allowlist.

**Format rule.** Map the device's free-text `sdCard.type` to a set of accepted formats: `microsd`, `sd`, `cfexpress-a`, `cfexpress-b`, `cfast`, `xqd`. Map each card from its `type` field (`microSD`, `SD`, `microSD Express`) or, for CFexpress/CFast/XQD cards, from its name. A recommended card must be in the device's set.
- Watch the free text: "microSD UHS-I (UHS-II Compatible)", "Dual slots: CFexpress Type A or SD UHS-II", "SDHC (officially up to 32GB, SDXC works unofficially)", "Dual CFexpress Type B (XQD compatible)". Strip "microsd…" before testing for a plain "sd" so microSD devices don't also match SD.
- Skip devices with `minSpeed` `"N/A"` (no slot) and Navigation SD cards.
- microSD Express cards count as microSD for format purposes.

**Speed rule.** Only for SD and microSD cards (CFexpress/CFast/XQD use VPG ratings; skip them).
- Device minimum = the **first** speed token in `minSpeed` **before** any parenthesis or the word "recommended". "Class 10 / U1 (V30 recommended for video)" means U1, not V30. "V90 (SD), VPG400 (CFexpress)" means V90.
- Rank: `C10 = U1 = V10` < `U3 = V30` < `V60` < `V90`.
- Card speed = `specs.speedClass`. Flag if card rank < device rank.

**Promotions.** Run the same two rules on every `appliesTo` entry in `data/promoted-cards.json`, and flag `appliesTo` ids that don't exist in `devices.json`.

**Allowlist.** `scripts/check-recommendations.allow.json`: `[{ device, card, reason }]`. Every entry needs a reason Pietro has agreed to.

**Sanity check of your output against the October 2 audit numbers:** 51 format problems, 25 speed problems (after removing the false positives the plan describes), plus in promotions: `kingston-canvas-react-sd` on `nikon-z9` (wrong format), and dead ids `fujifilm-x-t5` (real id `fujifilm-xt5`) and `dji-phantom-4-pro-v2`. Your counts may differ a little depending on edge cases. Look at every difference, don't tune the script to hit the number.
